The mobile app is the part of field service software your techs use most. If it is bad, they will fight you on it. Here is what techs actually need.

**A clear daily view**

Techs want to open the app and see their day. Jobs listed in order with addresses, contact names, and estimated times. No digging through menus.

**One-tap navigation**

The app should open maps or directions with one tap. Techs should not have to copy addresses into Google Maps manually.

**Photo capture**

Techs need to take photos of work before and after. The app should handle this without leaving the job screen. Photos should upload automatically when signal is available.

**Payment collection**

Collecting payments on site avoids the need to chase invoices later. The app should take credit cards and process them immediately.

**Parts and materials logging**

When a tech uses parts, they should log them in the app. The system should update inventory and add the parts to the invoice.

**Offline mode**

Basements, tunnels, and rural areas kill cell signals. The app should work offline and sync when the tech gets back online.

**Customer signatures**

Collecting signatures on site provides proof of service. The app should support this with a finger or stylus.

**Push notifications**

Techs should get notified when a job changes, when a new job is assigned, and when a customer sends a message. No one should have to keep checking the app.
