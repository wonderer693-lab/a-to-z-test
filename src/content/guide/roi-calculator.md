Field service software costs money. The question is whether it saves more than it costs. In most cases, the answer is yes by a wide margin. Here is how to calculate your potential savings.

**Fewer missed appointments**

Automated reminders reduce no-shows by 30% to 50%. If you have ten appointments a day and each one is worth $150 in service call revenue, a 30% reduction in no-shows saves you $450 per day.

**Faster invoicing**

Sending invoices from the field instead of processing them in the office later saves 5 to 10 minutes per job. For a business doing 20 jobs per day, that is 2 to 3 hours of office time saved daily.

**More jobs per day**

Better dispatching means techs spend less time driving between jobs. Even saving 15 minutes per tech per day adds about one extra job per week per tech.

**Payment speed**

Built-in payment processing means customers pay faster. Getting paid in days instead of weeks improves cash flow and reduces time spent chasing payments.

**Sample calculation**

For a business with 5 techs doing 20 jobs per day:
- Fewer no-shows: $450/day
- Faster invoicing: $60/day in office time
- More jobs: $200/day in additional revenue
- Faster payments: Reduced receivables by 15 days

Total estimated benefit: $710/day or roughly $15,000 per month. Even with software costs of $200 to $500 per month, the ROI is clear.
