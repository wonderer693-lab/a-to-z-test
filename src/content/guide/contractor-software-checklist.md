Before you commit to a field service platform, ask these questions. The answers will tell you if the tool is a good fit.

**Scheduling and dispatching**

1. Does the schedule show all my techs at once?
2. Can I drag and drop jobs to reassign them?
3. Does it show travel time between jobs?
4. Can customers book their own appointments online?

**Invoicing and payments**

5. Can I send invoices from the mobile app?
6. Does it accept credit card payments on site?
7. Does it sync with QuickBooks automatically?
8. Can I set up recurring invoices for maintenance agreements?

**Mobile features**

9. Does the mobile app work offline?
10. Can techs capture signatures and photos?
11. Does it provide GPS directions to job sites?
12. Can techs log parts and materials used?

**Customer management**

13. Does it store customer history including past jobs?
14. Can I send automated appointment reminders?
15. Does it have a customer portal for self-service?

**Reporting**

16. Can I see job profitability per tech?
17. Does it track key metrics like first-time fix rate?
18. Can I export data for my accountant?

**Support and training**

19. What kind of onboarding support is included?
20. Is phone support available during business hours?
