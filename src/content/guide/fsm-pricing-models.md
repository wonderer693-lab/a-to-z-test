Field service software pricing can be confusing. Vendors use different models and most do not publish their prices online. Here is what you need to know.

**Per-tech pricing**

Some tools charge per technician per month. If you have five techs, you pay for five seats. This model scales with your business. The downside is that your bill goes up every time you hire. Examples: Housecall Pro, Jobber.

**Flat-rate pricing**

A few vendors charge a flat monthly fee regardless of how many techs you have. This makes budgeting simple. Your bill stays the same whether you have three techs or thirty. Examples: Smart Service, RazorSync.

**Tiered pricing**

Most tools offer multiple tiers. The base plan covers the essentials. Higher tiers unlock features like marketing automation, advanced reporting, or GPS tracking. You pay more as your needs grow. Examples: ServiceTitan, mHelpDesk.

**What you get at each price point**

Under $100 per month: Scheduling, basic invoicing, customer management. Suitable for solo operators and two-person crews.

$100 to $250 per month: Everything above plus mobile apps, payment processing, client communication tools, basic reporting. Suitable for crews of 2-10 techs.

$250 to $500 per month: Advanced dispatching, marketing automation, detailed reporting, multiple integrations. Suitable for 10+ tech operations.

Over $500 per month: Enterprise features, custom integrations, dedicated account management, API access. Suitable for large multi-location operations.
