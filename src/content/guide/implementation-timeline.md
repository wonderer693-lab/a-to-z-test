Implementing field service software takes longer than most contractors expect. Here is a realistic timeline based on what we have seen work.

**Week 1: Setup and configuration**

Create your account. Set up your company profile, add your techs, configure your service areas. Import your customer list. Connect your accounting software. Most vendors have onboarding support for this phase.

**Week 2: Data migration**

Move your customer history, job records, and equipment data into the new system. This takes longer if you are coming from another software. Plan for a few days of cleanup work.

**Week 3: Training**

Train your office staff first. They need to learn scheduling, dispatching, and reporting before the techs start using the mobile app. Then train your techs in a group session. Follow up with individual support for anyone struggling.

**Week 4: Parallel run**

Run both systems simultaneously for a week. Keep your old system as a backup while your team gets comfortable with the new one. This catches issues before they affect customers.

**Week 5: Go live**

Cut over to the new system. Monitor closely for the first week. Have your vendor support contact ready for questions.

**Total timeline: 4 to 6 weeks**

Complex tools like ServiceTitan can take 8 to 12 weeks. Simpler tools like Housecall Pro can be running in 2 weeks. Plan for the longer timeline and you will be set either way.
