Customer management in field service software goes beyond a digital address book. The best tools give you a complete view of every customer interaction.

**Customer history**

Every job, invoice, and communication gets logged to the customer record. When a customer calls, you see their entire history on one screen. The equipment you installed, when you serviced it last, what they paid, any issues they had. No more asking the customer to repeat themselves.

**Automated reminders**

Customers forget appointments. Software does not. Automated text and email reminders cut no-shows by 30% to 50%. The system sends a reminder the day before and another one the morning of.

**Customer portal**

Many tools offer a portal where customers can book appointments, view their history, and pay invoices. This reduces phone calls and gives customers control over their experience.

**Lead tracking**

Some tools track leads from first contact through to completed job. You can see where leads come from, what they were quoted, and whether they booked. This tells you which marketing channels are working.

**Communication log**

Every text, email, and phone call gets logged to the customer record. If a customer claims they never received a notification, you have proof that it was sent.
