Reporting is where field service software earns its keep. The data you collect every day becomes useful when it is summarized into reports that show you how your business is performing.

**Revenue reports**

The most basic report shows revenue by day, week, or month. Filter by tech, by job type, or by customer. See trends over time. Know which months are your busiest and plan accordingly.

**Job profitability**

This is the most valuable report and the one most contractors do not run. It compares what you charged for a job to what it actually cost you in labor, parts, and travel. If you consistently lose money on certain job types, this report tells you.

**Tech performance**

See which techs complete the most jobs per day, have the highest customer satisfaction, and generate the most revenue. Also see which ones have the most callbacks.

**Customer reports**

Identify your most valuable customers, customers who have not booked in a while, and customers with overdue invoices. Use this data to target your marketing and collections efforts.

**First-time fix rate**

This metric tracks how often a job gets resolved on the first visit. A low rate indicates that techs lack the right parts, skills, or information. Improving this metric has a direct impact on profitability.
