Scheduling and dispatching are the heart of field service software. Everything else supports these functions. Here is what to look for.

**The dispatch board**

The best dispatch boards show all your techs on one screen. Jobs appear as blocks on a timeline. You can drag jobs between techs to reassign them. Color coding shows job status at a glance. Confirmed jobs are green. In progress is blue. Overdue is red.

**Auto-scheduling**

Some tools can assign jobs automatically based on tech skills, location, and availability. This saves the dispatcher time but takes trust to use. Most contractors start with manual scheduling and move to auto-scheduling as they get comfortable.

**Travel time tracking**

Good software accounts for travel time between jobs. If a tech finishes early, the system shows the closest next job. This adds up to more jobs per day.

**Route optimization**

For multi-stop days, route optimization arranges jobs in the most efficient order. This cuts drive time and fuel costs. Not all tools offer this. It is worth paying extra for if your techs drive between multiple jobs each day.

**Real-time updates**

When a job status changes in the field, the dispatch board should update immediately. The office team can see where each tech is without calling them.
