Inventory tracking is one of the most overlooked features in field service software. Most tools treat it as an afterthought. A few do it well.

**Basic inventory tracking**

Most FSM tools let you track parts inventory at a basic level. You add parts to a job and the system deducts them from stock. This works for small operations that carry limited inventory.

**Advanced inventory management**

Tools like RazorSync and ServiceTitan offer advanced features. Barcode scanning speeds up receiving and counting. Purchase orders track what you ordered and when it arrives. Vendor management keeps supplier information organized.

**Truck stock management**

For contractors who keep parts on trucks, some tools let you track inventory by vehicle. Each tech has their own inventory list. When they use a part on a job, it gets deducted from their truck stock.

**Reordering**

The most advanced systems track minimum stock levels and generate purchase orders automatically when inventory runs low. This prevents the situation where a tech shows up for a job without the right parts.

**What most contractors need**

If you carry less than 200 part numbers, basic inventory tracking is probably enough. If you manage thousands of parts across multiple trucks and locations, you need a tool with advanced inventory features.
