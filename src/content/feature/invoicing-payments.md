Invoicing and payment processing are where field service software pays for itself. Getting paid faster is the single biggest financial benefit of going digital.

**Mobile invoicing**

The best tools let techs create and send invoices from the job site. They enter the work performed and the parts used. The system calculates the total. The customer gets an email or text with a pay button. Money arrives before the tech leaves the driveway.

**Payment processing**

Built-in payment processing means you do not need a separate merchant account. Rates vary by provider. Most tools charge around 2.5% per transaction. Some charge a flat monthly fee instead. Compare rates carefully. They add up.

**Recurring invoices**

For maintenance agreements and service contracts, recurring invoices save time. Set up the schedule once. The system generates and sends invoices automatically.

**Deposits and milestone billing**

For large commercial jobs, you might need deposits or milestone payments. Some tools support this. Most do not. If you do commercial work, ask about this specifically.

**ACH payments**

Credit cards are convenient but cost 2% to 3% in processing fees. ACH payments cost a flat fee of around $0.50. Give customers the option to pay either way.
