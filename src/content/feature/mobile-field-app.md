The mobile app is the face of your field service software for your technicians. If they hate it, the whole system fails. Here is what makes a mobile app work in the real world.

**Speed matters most**

A slow mobile app destroys adoption. Techs are impatient. They do not want to wait for screens to load while a customer watches. The best apps launch in under two seconds and navigate between screens instantly.

**Offline is essential**

Cell service is unreliable in basements, commercial buildings, and rural areas. A good mobile app downloads job data when there is signal and works offline. Changes sync when the tech gets back online. No data is lost.

**Simple navigation**

Techs should be able to do their job with two or three taps. See today's jobs. Select a job. See the address, customer name, and job details. Everything else is secondary.

**Built-in communication**

Push notifications beat checking the app. When a job changes, the tech gets notified. When a customer sends a message, it shows up immediately. The tech should never miss a change because they did not open the app.

**Customer experience features**

The best apps let techs send photos and status updates to customers automatically. When a tech is on the way, the customer gets a notification with an ETA and the tech's name. This reduces calls to the office asking "when will they be here?"
