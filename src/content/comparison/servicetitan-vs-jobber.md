This comparison is straightforward because ServiceTitan and Jobber target completely different markets.

ServiceTitan is for established contracting businesses with ten or more techs. It offers a complete platform with dispatch, CRM, marketing, reporting, and accounting integrations. You pay for that comprehensiveness with a higher price and a longer setup process.

Jobber is for smaller teams that value simplicity and client relationships. It costs less, is easier to learn, and has a clean interface that does not overwhelm new users.

The deciding factor is your team size and budget. Under ten techs and under $200 a month, Jobber is the obvious choice. Over ten techs with budget for a premium tool, ServiceTitan has more to offer.
