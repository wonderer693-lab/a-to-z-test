Commercial HVAC work is different from residential. Jobs are larger, schedules are more complex, compliance requirements are stricter, and the contracts involve maintenance agreements that need consistent tracking.

ServiceTitan handles commercial HVAC well. The dispatch board manages multi-day projects across multiple crews. The maintenance agreement module tracks contract renewals and schedules preventive visits automatically. Reporting shows job profitability at the commercial account level. It is the most expensive option but the most complete.

FieldEdge has strong flat rate pricing tools that work for commercial service and repair. The offline mobile app is useful for commercial sites with limited connectivity. The interface is older but the core functionality is reliable.

ServiceFusion integrates well with flat rate pricing providers and has a maintenance agreement module that tracks commercial contracts. It costs less than ServiceTitan but has fewer features.

For commercial HVAC contractors, ServiceTitan is the recommended choice. It costs more but the commercial-specific features justify the premium.
