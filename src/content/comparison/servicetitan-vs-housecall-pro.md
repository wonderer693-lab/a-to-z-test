ServiceTitan and Housecall Pro are two of the biggest names in field service software, but they serve very different audiences. Choosing between them mostly comes down to the size of your business.

ServiceTitan is built for larger operations with ten or more techs. It has everything: smart dispatching, marketing automation, advanced reporting, deep QuickBooks integration. It also has a price tag to match. You are looking at around $400 per month minimum, and it goes up from there. The learning curve is steep. Plan for weeks of onboarding.

Housecall Pro is built for smaller crews. It costs less, starts faster, and the mobile experience is better out of the box. You can sign up and start scheduling jobs the same day. Techs get a clean mobile app that handles payments, confirms appointments, and sends automatic updates. The tradeoff is that the feature set is shallower.

If you have more than ten techs and complex scheduling needs, ServiceTitan justifies its cost. If you have one to ten techs, Housecall Pro will do everything you need at a fraction of the price.
