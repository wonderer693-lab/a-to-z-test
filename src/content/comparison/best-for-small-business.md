Running a small crew means you wear every hat. Scheduler, dispatcher, marketer, accountant. You need software that handles the basics without requiring a week to set up.

For one to five tech crews, the choice comes down to three tools. Housecall Pro leads the group with a strong mobile app, built-in payment processing, and quick setup. You can be up and running in an afternoon.

Jobber is a close second with better client management features. If your business runs on repeat customers and referrals, the extra detail in customer history matters.

Kickserv is the budget option at $49 a month. It covers scheduling, invoicing, and dispatching. The feature set is thinner, but the price is hard to beat for a solo operator.

Between the three, Housecall Pro delivers the best overall experience for the price. Start there unless you have a specific reason to choose one of the others.
