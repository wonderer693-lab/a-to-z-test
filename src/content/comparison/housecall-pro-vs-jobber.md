Housecall Pro and Jobber are the two most popular choices for small contractors. Both are good products. The difference comes down to how you work.

Housecall Pro has the better mobile experience. The tech app is fast, intuitive, and handles payments on site without friction. Invoicing and payment processing are deeply integrated. Customers get emailed invoices with a pay button. Money lands in your account faster than with most competitors.

Jobber has stronger client management. Customer history, communication logs, and job histories are more detailed. If your business depends on long-term client relationships and repeat work, Jobber gives you better tools to manage those connections.

Pricing is similar. Housecall Pro starts at $69 a month. Jobber starts at $49 a month. Both have mid-tier plans around $129 to $149 that most contractors need.

Choose Housecall Pro if field techs doing payments on site is a priority. Choose Jobber if client relationship management matters more.
