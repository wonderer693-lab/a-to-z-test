Jobber and FieldEdge approach field service management from different angles. Jobber is built around client relationships and ease of use. FieldEdge focuses on dispatching power and offline mobile capabilities.

Jobber wins on interface and price. It starts at $49 a month. The layout is clean. New users can navigate it without training. Client management is the standout feature. Every interaction lives in one place. If your business relies on repeat customers and referrals, that makes a difference.

FieldEdge has stronger dispatching for multi-crew operations. The drag and drop timeline is more capable when you manage several crews across different areas. The mobile app works offline. That matters for plumbers and electricians working in basements and commercial buildings with no signal.

FieldEdge costs more and looks dated. The interface has not kept up with newer tools.

Choose Jobber for one to ten techs where customer relationships drive the business. Choose FieldEdge for larger crews where dispatching power and offline access matter more than a modern UI.
