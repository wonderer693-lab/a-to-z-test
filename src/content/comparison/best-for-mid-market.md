Mid-market contractors face a different set of problems than small crews. Multiple dispatchers, several crews running simultaneously, complex scheduling constraints, and a need for detailed reporting. The tools that work for a three-person operation break down at this scale.

ServiceTitan is the best fit for most mid-market contractors. It handles complex dispatching across multiple crews. The reporting gives you visibility into job costs, tech performance, and customer trends. The marketing automation helps fill the pipeline. It costs more, but at this scale the ROI is clear.

FieldAware competes on multi-location support. If you have branches in different cities, FieldAware lets you manage them from one account with separate dispatch boards and consolidated reporting. It is harder to set up but more flexible for distributed operations.

FieldEdge has strong offline capabilities for crews working in areas with poor cell coverage. The dispatch board is capable for this size of operation.

For most mid-market contractors, ServiceTitan is where to start unless multi-location management or offline access are specific dealbreakers.
