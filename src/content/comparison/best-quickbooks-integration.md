QuickBooks is the accounting backbone for most contractors. Your field service software either works well with it or becomes a source of headaches. We tested the QuickBooks integration on the most popular platforms to see which ones actually work.

ServiceTitan has the best integration. It syncs in both directions. Invoices created in ServiceTitan appear in QuickBooks. Payments posted in QuickBooks show up in ServiceTitan. Customer data stays in sync. Setup takes a few hours with support from both teams.

Housecall Pro's integration is solid for smaller operations. It sends invoices and payments to QuickBooks reliably. The main limitation is that it is mostly one-way. Changes made in QuickBooks do not always flow back to Housecall Pro.

Fergus handles QuickBooks sync well for plumbing and HVAC workflows. Job costing data transfers correctly, which is important for contractors who track profitability by job.

For deep two-way sync that accounting teams can trust, ServiceTitan is the clear winner. For smaller operations where occasional manual reconciliation is acceptable, Housecall Pro works fine.
