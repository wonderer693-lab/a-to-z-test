ServiceFusion comes from the HVAC world and it shows. The flat rate pricing integration is better than anything else in this price range. If your business uses flat rate books for pricing repairs and replacements, this tool handles that workflow without making you juggle multiple systems.

The dispatch board gives you a view of where each tech is and what they are working on. Jobs show estimated times, actual time on site, and flags for anything that needs attention.

QuickBooks integration is one of the more solid implementations we have tested. Invoices sync in both directions. Payments post correctly. Your accountant will not find discrepancies between the two systems.

Customer support is a genuine strength. When we had questions during testing, response times were under an hour during business hours. The support team knows the product and knows HVAC workflows.

The mobile app works but is not as polished as Housecall Pro or Jobber. Techs can see their schedule, enter job details, and collect payments, but the interface lags behind the competition.

ServiceFusion fits best for HVAC and plumbing companies that rely on flat rate pricing and need reliable dispatching for crews in the 3-15 tech range.
