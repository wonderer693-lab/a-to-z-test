mHelpDesk sits in a comfortable middle ground. It does not have the brand recognition of ServiceTitan or the simplicity of Housecall Pro, but it covers most of what a contracting business needs at a price that falls between the two.

The asset management feature is something most competitors either do poorly or leave out entirely. You can track equipment by customer, log maintenance history, and set up warranty tracking. That matters for contractors who service boilers, AC units, or water heaters and need to know what was installed and when.

The dispatch board handles multiple crews well. You can set up different teams, assign jobs by skill set, and see conflicts before they happen.

Pricing starts at $85 a month for the basic plan. The Professional plan at $145 adds the mobile app and customer portal. The Business plan at $205 includes asset management and advanced reporting.

The mobile app is functional but not fast. Techs reported lag when loading job details on site. That is frustrating when you are standing in front of a customer.

mHelpDesk is a good fit if you manage 2 to 20 techs and need asset tracking that the cheaper tools do not offer.
