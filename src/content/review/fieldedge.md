FieldEdge has been around long enough that a lot of HVAC and plumbing contractors know the name. It started as a desktop tool and moved online a few years back. The dispatch board is where it shines. You see every tech on a timeline. Jobs show up as blocks you can drag to reassign. Color coding tells you which jobs are running late, which need parts, which are emergencies.

The mobile app works even when the tech does not have signal. Jobs sync when they get back online. That matters for plumbers who spend time in basements and crawl spaces where cell reception is spotty.

The billing side handles flat rate pricing, which is common in HVAC. You can set up price books and push invoices from the job. Payments post to QuickBooks if you use that for accounting.

The downsides are hard to ignore. The interface looks like it was designed ten years ago. It gets the job done but does not feel modern. Setup takes time, especially if you have a lot of historical data to bring over. Integration options are limited compared to newer platforms.

FieldEdge is a solid choice for HVAC and plumbing companies that manage multiple crews and need reliable dispatching. If having a modern interface matters to you, look elsewhere.
