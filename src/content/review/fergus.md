Fergus started in the UK plumbing market and expanded to the US a few years ago. The plumbing and HVAC focus is baked into every feature. Job costing, for example, tracks parts, labor, and overhead against each job so you know exactly what a job actually cost you.

The CRM side tracks leads from first contact through to completed job. You can see where each lead came from, what they were quoted, and whether they booked. That is useful for contractors doing their own marketing and wanting to know what works.

The mobile app is fast. It loads job details, customer history, and maps without the lag we see in some other apps. Techs can start and stop timers, log parts used, and collect payments on site.

On the downside, some features feel UK-centric even on the US version. VAT handling, address formats, and some terminology default to British conventions. You can work around it, but it adds friction.

Pricing runs $79 to $279 a month depending on features and tech count. The mid-tier plan at $149 is where most contractors end up.

Fergus is worth a look for plumbing and HVAC contractors who want strong job costing and do not mind a tool that was built overseas.
