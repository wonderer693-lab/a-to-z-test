RazorSync stands out for its inventory management. Most field service tools treat inventory as an afterthought. RazorSync has barcode scanning, parts tracking, purchase orders, and vendor management. If your business carries significant inventory, this matters more than a pretty interface.

Unlimited users is a rare perk. Every plan includes unlimited office staff and techs. For a growing company, that removes the headache of adding users and watching the bill go up.

The scheduling board is functional but busy. Too many fields and options crowd the screen. Once you learn where everything lives, it works fine, but first impressions are rough.

The mobile app syncs jobs and lets techs update status, log parts, and collect payments. We saw occasional delays in syncing, especially in areas with weak cell coverage. Nothing that loses data, just frustrating when you need information right now.

Pricing runs $99 to $249 a month. The mid-tier Pro plan at $149 is the sweet spot for most contractors.

RazorSync is a practical choice for contractors who carry parts inventory and want a tool that tracks it properly, even if the interface takes some getting used to.
