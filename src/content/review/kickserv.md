Kickserv does not try to do everything and that is part of the appeal. It is a straightforward tool for contractors who need to schedule jobs, send invoices, and keep track of customers without paying for features they will never use.

The price is the main draw. Plans start at $49 a month for a single user. That includes scheduling, invoicing, customer management, and the mobile app. Most small contractors can get what they need from the $99 plan.

The mobile app lets techs see their schedule, navigate to jobs, collect payments, and send invoices from the field. It is not as polished as Housecall Pro's app, but it covers the basics.

GPS tracking is included on the Plus plan. You can see where your techs are on a map and verify they arrived at the job site.

The tradeoff is that Kickserv is simpler across the board. Reporting is basic. There is no marketing automation. No customer portal. If those things matter, you will need to look at pricier options.

Kickserv works best for solo operators and two-person crews who need the essentials at a price that does not hurt.
