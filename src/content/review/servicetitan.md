ServiceTitan is the gorilla in the field service management room. If you have a ten-person crew and you need software that can keep up with everything that involves, this is probably the first name you will hear from other contractors.

The dispatch board is the standout feature. Drag a job from one tech to another and the system adjusts the schedule in real time. It shows travel time, parts availability, and whether the tech has the right certifications for the job. You can see your whole day on one screen without flipping between tabs.

Pricing is where people get tripped up. ServiceTitan does not publish numbers on their site. You have to talk to sales. Expect to pay somewhere around $400 per month as a starting point, and it goes up from there depending on features and tech count. That puts it out of reach for smaller operations, but for a busy shop with ten or more techs, the ROI tends to justify itself within the first year.

QuickBooks integration works well. Invoices sync automatically. Payments post to both systems. Your bookkeeper does not have to re-enter data from one platform to the other.

The learning curve is real. Plan on spending a couple weeks getting your team up to speed. ServiceTitan offers training and onboarding support, but it takes time. If you are switching from paper or from a simpler tool, budget for that transition period.

We recommend ServiceTitan for established businesses that have outgrown simpler tools. If you have five techs or fewer, there are cheaper options that will do what you need without the overhead.
