Jobber is built around the idea that knowing your customers matters. Every interaction with a client lives in one place: their contact info, job history, invoices, notes from the tech who visited last time. If you run a small operation and your business depends on repeat customers and referrals, this approach makes sense.

The interface is one of the cleanest in this category. Menus are where you expect them. The schedule board shows your day without clutter. New hires can figure out the basics in an hour or two.

Pricing starts at $49 a month for the Core plan. That gets you scheduling, invoicing, and client management. You will need the Connect plan at $129 for the mobile app and client communication features. Most small contractors land on Connect and find it covers what they need.

The client portal lets customers request jobs, view their history, and pay invoices online. That cuts down on phone calls and back-and-forth emails.

Jobber does not have the advanced dispatching or inventory features that larger operations need. If you manage more than five techs, the limitations start showing. But for a one to five person crew, it hits a sweet spot between price and capability.
