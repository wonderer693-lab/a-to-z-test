Smart Service is one of those tools that has been around since before cloud software was the norm. It started as a desktop application and the online version still reflects those roots. That is not all bad. The desktop version is fast and feature complete. You can do everything from scheduling to inventory management without switching screens.

The per-user pricing model is unusual in a good way. You pay a flat monthly fee for the whole company, not per tech. That makes the math simple for growing businesses.

Scheduling and dispatching are handled well. The board shows jobs by day and tech. You can print route sheets for crews that prefer paper, which some older contractors still want.

The mobile app is the weak spot. It shows jobs and lets techs update status, but it does not have the polish or speed of the mobile-first competitors. Field techs who rely heavily on their phones will notice the difference.

Smart Service works best for contractors who do most of their office work on a desktop and have field crews that do not need a lot of mobile functionality. It is functional software, it just has not kept up with the modern design trends.
