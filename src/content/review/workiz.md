Workiz puts customer communication at the center of everything. When a job gets scheduled, the system sends an automatic confirmation. The day before, a reminder goes out. The morning of, another one with the tech name and photo. Customers can text back directly without calling the office.

That kind of automation cuts down on no-shows and last-minute cancellations. Contractors we talked to said their missed appointment rate dropped by half after switching to Workiz.

Lead management is built into the same system. Incoming calls and online requests get logged as leads. You can quote them from the app and convert to a job in a few taps.

The mobile app is solid. Techs see their day, navigate to jobs, communicate with customers, and collect payments.

The missing piece is inventory management. Workiz does not track parts or stock. If your business depends on managing inventory, you will need a separate system or look elsewhere.

Pricing runs $99 to $249 per month. The mid-tier Professional plan at $149 covers most of what a small to mid-size crew needs.

Workiz is a strong pick for service businesses that want to reduce no-shows and keep customers informed without manual phone calls.
