These templates are designed to help contractors standardize their paperwork. You can customize them for your business.

## Service Report Template

A standard service report includes customer information, equipment details, work performed, parts used, and recommendations. Using a consistent format makes it easier to reference past work.

## Estimate Template

An estimate should clearly list the work to be performed, the cost of labor and materials, the total price, and the validity period. Include your license number and insurance information.

## Maintenance Agreement Template

A maintenance agreement covers the scope of work, schedule of visits, pricing, cancellation terms, and liability. Having a standard template ensures all agreements include the same terms.

## Inspection Checklist

Daily vehicle inspection, equipment inspection, and job site safety inspection checklists help ensure nothing is missed. Customize these for your specific equipment and trade.
