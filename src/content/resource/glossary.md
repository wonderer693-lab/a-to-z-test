**Dispatch board**: A visual interface that shows all scheduled jobs and technician assignments on one screen. Dispatchers can see conflicts, reassign jobs, and track progress.

**Field service management (FSM)**: Software that helps service businesses manage scheduling, dispatching, invoicing, and customer relationships.

**First-time fix rate**: The percentage of jobs resolved on the first visit. A higher rate means better prepared techs and fewer repeat visits.

**Flat rate pricing**: A pricing model where services are sold at a fixed price rather than by the hour. Common in HVAC and plumbing.

**GPS tracking**: A feature that shows technician locations on a map in real time. Used for dispatching and verifying arrival times.

**Job costing**: Tracking the actual costs of a job including labor, parts, travel, and overhead. Used to determine profitability.

**Maintenance agreement**: A contract for regular preventive service visits. Also called a service agreement or service contract.

**Mobile app**: The smartphone application technicians use in the field to view jobs, collect payments, and communicate with the office.

**Offline mode**: The ability to use the mobile app without an internet connection. Data syncs when connectivity is restored.

**Online booking**: A customer-facing feature that allows clients to schedule appointments through a website or portal.

**Payment processing**: The ability to accept credit card and ACH payments through the software. Usually includes invoicing and receipt features.

**QuickBooks integration**: A connection between the field service software and QuickBooks accounting software that syncs customer data, invoices, and payments.

**Route optimization**: A feature that arranges jobs in the most efficient order to minimize driving time between appointments.

**Scheduling board**: See dispatch board.

**Service agreement**: See maintenance agreement.

**Two-way sync**: An integration where data flows in both directions between two systems. Changes made in either system appear in the other.
