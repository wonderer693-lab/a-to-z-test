## How much does field service software cost?

Most field service tools range from $49 to $500 per month. The price depends on the number of techs, the features you need, and the vendor. Small contractors typically pay $100 to $200 per month. Larger operations pay $300 to $500 per month or more.

## What is the best field service software for small contractors?

For one to five tech crews, Housecall Pro is our top recommendation. It combines ease of use, a strong mobile app, and built-in payment processing at a reasonable price. Jobber is a close second and better for businesses that focus on client relationships.

## Can I use field service software on my phone?

Yes. Every major field service platform offers a mobile app for technicians. The best ones work offline, which is important for jobs in areas with poor cell coverage.

## How long does it take to set up field service software?

Simple tools like Housecall Pro or Jobber can be up and running in a day. Complex tools like ServiceTitan take several weeks. Most tools fall somewhere in between. Plan for two to four weeks.

## Does field service software integrate with QuickBooks?

Most field service tools integrate with QuickBooks. The quality of the integration varies. Higher-end tools offer two-way sync. Lower-priced tools typically offer one-way sync from the field service tool to QuickBooks.

## What features should I look for?

The essential features are scheduling and dispatching, invoicing and payments, a mobile app for techs, and customer management. Beyond that, look for features that match your specific needs: inventory tracking for parts-heavy businesses, marketing automation for growth-focused companies, and compliance tracking for regulated trades.

## Can I try field service software before buying?

Yes. Every major FSM platform offers a free trial, usually 14 to 30 days. Use the trial period to test the software with your actual workflows and get feedback from your team.
