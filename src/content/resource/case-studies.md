## From Paper to Profit: How One HVAC Company Saved 15 Hours a Week

A five-person HVAC company in Ohio was still using paper work orders and manual invoicing. The owner spent every Saturday catching up on paperwork. After switching to Housecall Pro, the office manager estimated they saved 15 hours per week. Invoices that used to take three days to send were being sent from the job site. Customers paid faster. The owner got his weekends back.

## Growing from 3 to 15 Techs Without Adding Office Staff

A plumbing company in Texas was growing fast and drowning in paperwork. The owner was afraid to hire more techs because the office could not handle the additional workload. After implementing ServiceTitan, they grew from 3 to 15 techs without adding office staff. The system automated scheduling, invoicing, and customer communication. The same team that handled three techs now handles fifteen.

## Cutting No-Shows by 60% with Automated Reminders

An electrical contractor in Florida was losing $3,000 per month to missed appointments. Customers would forget, no one would call to confirm, and techs would sit idle. After switching to Workiz with automated text reminders, their no-show rate dropped by 60%. The cost of the software was covered by the reduction in lost appointments alone.
